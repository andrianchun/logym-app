=== EXERCISE: Incline Dumbbell Press ===
ID: Incline_Dumbbell_Press
Target Muscles: upper chest (clavicular head), front deltoids, triceps
Equipment: pair of dumbbells and 30-45 degree incline gym bench
Layout: 1:1 Square Asymmetric Multi-Angle 3D (Top Hero)

[CARA MELAKUKAN (ID)]:
Berbaringlah di incline bench dengan masing-masing tangan memegang dumbbell di atas paha, telapak tangan saling berhadapan.
Gunakan dorongan paha untuk membantu mengangkat dumbbell satu per satu hingga sejajar dengan bahu.
Setelah dumbbell berada di ketinggian bahu, putar pergelangan tangan ke depan sehingga telapak tangan menghadap ke depan (menjauhi tubuh). Ini adalah posisi awal Anda.
Jaga kontrol penuh atas dumbbell setiap saat. Hembuskan napas dan dorong dumbbell ke atas menggunakan otot dada.
Luruskan tangan di atas, tahan sejenak, lalu turunkan beban secara perlahan. Tips: Idealnya, waktu menurunkan beban adalah dua kali lebih lama daripada saat mengangkatnya.
Ulangi gerakan ini sesuai jumlah repetisi yang ditentukan.
Setelah selesai, letakkan kembali dumbbell ke paha lalu ke lantai. Ini adalah cara paling aman untuk melepaskan dumbbell.

[INSTRUCTIONS (EN)]:
Lie back on an incline bench with a dumbbell in each hand atop your thighs. The palms of your hands will be facing each other.
Then, using your thighs to help push the dumbbells up, lift the dumbbells one at a time so that you can hold them at shoulder width.
Once you have the dumbbells raised to shoulder width, rotate your wrists forward so that the palms of your hands are facing away from you. This will be your starting position.
Be sure to keep full control of the dumbbells at all times. Then breathe out and push the dumbbells up with your chest.
Lock your arms at the top, hold for a second, and then start slowly lowering the weight. Tip Ideally, lowering the weights should take about twice as long as raising them.
Repeat the movement for the prescribed amount of repetitions.
When you are done, place the dumbbells back on your thighs and then on the floor. This is the safest manner to release the dumbbells.
