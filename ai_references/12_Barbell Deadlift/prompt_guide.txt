=== EXERCISE: Barbell Deadlift ===
ID: Barbell_Deadlift
Target Muscles: erector spinae, glutes, hamstrings, traps, lats
Equipment: Olympic barbell with bumper plates on lifting platform
Layout: 1:1 Square Asymmetric Multi-Angle 3D (Left Hero)

[CARA MELAKUKAN (ID)]:
Berdirilah di depan barbel yang sudah dimuati beban.
Jaga punggung tetap lurus, tekuk lutut, condongkan tubuh ke depan, dan pegang bar dengan genggaman atas (overhand) selebar bahu. Ini adalah posisi awal. Tips: Jika genggaman sulit dipertahankan, gunakan genggaman campuran atau strap pergelangan tangan.
Sambil memegang bar, mulailah mengangkat dengan mendorong menggunakan kaki dan secara bersamaan menegakkan batang tubuh sambil membuang napas. Dalam posisi tegak, busungkan dada dan kontraksikan punggung dengan menarik tulang belikat ke belakang.
Kembali ke posisi awal dengan menekuk lutut dan mencondongkan tubuh ke depan di pinggul sambil tetap menjaga punggung lurus. Saat beban menyentuh lantai, Anda siap untuk repetisi berikutnya.
Lakukan repetisi sesuai jumlah yang ditentukan dalam program.

[INSTRUCTIONS (EN)]:
Stand in front of a loaded barbell.
While keeping the back as straight as possible, bend your knees, bend forward and grasp the bar using a medium (shoulder width) overhand grip. This will be the starting position of the exercise. Tip: If it is difficult to hold on to the bar with this grip, alternate your grip or use wrist straps.
While holding the bar, start the lift by pushing with your legs while simultaneously getting your torso to the upright position as you breathe out. In the upright position, stick your chest out and contract the back by bringing the shoulder blades back. Think of how the soldiers in the military look when they are in standing in attention.
Go back to the starting position by bending at the knees while simultaneously leaning the torso forward at the waist while keeping the back straight. When the weights on the bar touch the floor you are back at the starting position and ready to perform another repetition.
Perform the amount of repetitions prescribed in the program.
